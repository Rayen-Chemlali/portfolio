About Me

I'm in my final year at ENSEEIHT (INP Toulouse), on the HPC Big Data track, alongside the PSMSC Master (Scientific Computing). Before that, I did four years of software engineering at INSAT, in Tunisia.

LLMs and agentic AI are the field I have worked on the most: during my internship at Talan, in freelance work, and in my end-of-year projects, including a multi-agent travel planning system. HPC gave me a taste for reasoning close to the hardware: I optimised CUDA kernels to within 6% of cuBLAS. On the production side, at Plug & Tel, I improved an answering machine detection model running on live VoIP calls and built microservices in Go.

Competitive programming and hackathons taught me to break down a problem and to work in a team under pressure. The latest one: I won the Enedis track of the AI For Impact hackathon by La Mêlée, in October 2026.

I'm looking for a 6-month end-of-studies internship in 2027, ideally in R&D, in agentic AI, HPC, or both.
